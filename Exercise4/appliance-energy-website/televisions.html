Tips for Reducing Television Energy Use

Lower Brightness

Placeholder text: Reducing screen brightness from maximum can noticeably cut power draw without significantly affecting picture quality in most rooms.

Enable Eco/Power-Saving Mode

Placeholder text: Most modern televisions include an eco mode that automatically adjusts brightness and backlight based on the room's ambient light.

Avoid Standby Power Drain

Placeholder text: Turning a television off at the wall (rather than leaving it on standby) avoids small but constant "phantom" power use over time.
